# Incident Response

## Use this skill when
- Coordinating during a live incident
- Triage, containment, and stabilization
- Running post-incident review

## Do not use this skill when
- Routine support tickets
- Long-term investigations (postmortems separately)

## Instructions
1. Designate a clear role: incident commander, comms, responders.
2. Stabilize first: stop the bleeding, contain blast radius.
3. Declare severity and inform stakeholders with updates.
4. Keep a timeline log of actions and evidence.
5. Focus on mitigation, defer root-cause in the heat.
6. Communicate status to users per policy.
7. Run a blameless post-incident review: timeline, causes, actions.
8. Convert findings into tracked follow-ups.

## Checklist
- Roles assigned
- Timeline recorded
- Comms published

## Output Format
- Severity | timeline | containment | postmortem | action items

## Common Mistakes
- Debugging heroically without coordination
- Silence to users during long incidents
- Skipping postmortem follow-through
